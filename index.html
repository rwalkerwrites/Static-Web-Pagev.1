Research Interests

My research interests include the rhetoric of health and medicine, feminist theories, tactical technical communication, and netnography. I am also interested in rhetorics of the body and how illness rhetorically influences personal and social identities. For my dissertation, I am planning to explore constitutive rhetoric in the context of digital health communities.

Education

I have a background in English and Technical Communication, with a research emphasis in medical communication and rhetoric.

My degrees include:
  • PhD in Technical Communication and Rhetoric, Texas Tech University (in progress)
  • Master's in English: Technical Communication, Minnesota State University, Mankato
  • Bachelor's in English Literature, Point Loma Nazarene University

Experience

I have over 15-years of experience in the field of medical communications. I started as a freelance medical writer pitching stories to health magazines before joining a company that develops clinical decision support tools. In my current role, I manage a team of writers and editors, analyze and synthesize evidence, draft and edit content, and collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure the accuracy and clarity of medical information for use at the point of care (the patient-clinician encounter).

Examples of projects I have been involved in recently include:

  • Developing an in-house style guide for use by medical writers and editors.
  • Creating an AI agent for summarizing clinical practice guidelines with an output that aligns with our style guide.
  • Working with a small, multidisciplinary team to develop recommendations for our product's UI/UX based on user testing and feedback from new and prospective customers.
